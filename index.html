Trip overview

Want to see the Busan that locals love? On our 1-Day Hidden Yeongdo Tour you cross the bridge from Nampo and follow one island from west to east: a cliff path above the sea, a seafood lunch with the haenyeo divers, a guided hike up Bongnaesan and a pebble beach at sunset.

Small groups of up to 10, led by founder Utsho, on foot and by local bus. No long transfers, just time to look around.

  • 1 day09:30 – 18:00
  • 3 placescoast · summit · beach
  • ≈ 8 kmon foot
  • Max 10people
  1. S
    Nampo Station, Exit 6 (Line 1)
    Meet your guide, then take a local bus across Yeongdo Bridge to the start of the coastal walk.
  2. 1
    Jeoryeong Coastal Walk
    Start at the mosaic wall and walk south along the sea, about 2 hours. The walk ends at Jungni, where we stop for a seafood lunch with the haenyeo divers.
  3. 2
    Bongnaesan
    A short ride up to the trailhead, then a guided hike to the 395 m summit and down the east side of the island.
  4. 3
    Gamji Beach
    Local bus towards Taejongdae, then the promenade, grilled clams and sunset. Buses run back to Nampo.

Want to plan it yourself? See all stops on the live map.

  • Shoes: trails and pebble beaches, so wear trainers or hiking shoes.
  • T-money card: bus fares are not included; charge your card before you come.
  • Rain plan: in heavy rain we skip the summit and add a café stop on the coast.
  • Respect locals: parts of the route pass homes; we keep groups small and quiet.
